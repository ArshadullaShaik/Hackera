Privacy & data

Privacy policy

HACKERA values your privacy. Learn how we handle data, cookies, and aggregated hackathon information.

Information we collect

HACKERA does not require an account to browse the hackathon index.

  • Aggregated hackathon data: publicly available event information from partner sites and public APIs.
  • Technical data: standard server access logs used to prevent abuse and monitor service health.

Cookies & local storage

We use minimal local or session storage to retain search filters, viewing preferences, and page state. We do not use invasive cross-site tracking cookies.

Third-party services

When you visit an external hackathon host or registration page, that provider's privacy policy applies. Review its policy before sharing personal data.
